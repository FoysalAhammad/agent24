Monostylistic preponderance per screen; ancillary accentual; decastylistic conflation proscribed.

## Inviolable Canons (All Ten)
- Pauciloquent density: recondite yet precise diction; minimal verbiage, maximal signification.
- ≥44dp targets; contrast-verified; non-chromatic reduplication (icon+text); content-descriptions; macrotypography; keyboard/focus (depth-ordered where stratified); reduced-motion honored.
- Tachytelic purposeful kinetics (opacity/transform); perpetuum mobile live-only; blur-quantity animation proscribed; code/terminal/output interiors perpetually solid legible; arcana never exposed.
- Legacy `Design-Skills/` preserved intact; this corpus additive, non-destructive.
