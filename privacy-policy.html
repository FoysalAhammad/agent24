Privacy Policy

This policy explains what user data the Agent 24 Android application accesses, collects, uses, handles and shares, with whom, and what control you have over it. The same policy is shown inside the app before you can use it.

Repository Full documentation

1. Overview & consent

Agent 24 ("the app") is a free, open-source Android application distributed under the MIT license. It has no user accounts and no registration. The app does not operate a backend for your conversations: there is no "Agent 24 server" that stores your chats.

On first launch the app displays this policy and asks for your consent with an "I Agree & Continue" button before anything is collected. You must take that affirmative action to proceed; navigating away does not count as consent. Android runtime permissions (accessibility, camera, storage and similar) are requested only when a feature you choose actually needs them, never before.

In short: your conversations and files stay on your device; only the AI provider you configured receives your prompts, over TLS. We do not sell any data.

3. Persistent device identifiers

The app does not collect or use the following identifiers, and does not link any persistent device identifier to personal or sensitive user data or to resettable device identifiers:

  • IMEI, IMSI and SIM serial numbers
  • Android Advertising ID (AAID)
  • App Set ID
  • Serial numbers or any other persistent hardware identifier

The app does not build cross-app profiles and does not combine identifier data with location, contacts or any sensitive category.

4. What is sent to AI providers

When you send a message, the app forwards the message text, conversation context and any tool results the model requested to the provider you selected in Settings → Models, over TLS. This happens only for the conversation you started — there is no background syncing of your chats.

  • The transfer is governed by the selected provider's own privacy policy (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google).
  • The project does not relay conversations through servers it operates.
  • Removing your API key (Settings → Models → Remove) stops all further calls immediately.
  • Model replies, reasoning display and streaming are produced by that provider under your key.

5. Third-party code and advertising

Components bundled with the app (PDF processing, on-device OCR, the terminal runtime) operate entirely on your device. No embedded SDK in the app sells personal or sensitive user data from this app.

Advertising: when advertisements are shown, they are delivered through the project's own remotely signed configuration. There is no third-party advertising SDK embedded in the app, no advertising identifier is collected, no cross-app advertising profile is built, and ad requests contain no personal or sensitive data. You can review advertising behaviour at any time in the app's privacy settings.

Updates and configuration: the app checks a signed configuration bundle (version metadata and optional ad placement) — these requests contain no personal data and are verifiable by signature.

7. Retention and deletion

Retention: your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. The project retains nothing on servers it operates, and no account exists — so there is no server-side copy of your conversations.

Deletion — you are always in control:

  • In-app: Settings → Delete All Data (removes sessions, history and keys)
  • Full removal: uninstall the app — app-private data is removed with it
  • Keep a copy first: Settings → Export Data (Markdown / JSON)
  • API keys only: Settings → Models → Remove

Because the app has no accounts, there is no separate account-deletion procedure: deleting the app data (above) deletes all associated data. Nothing is frozen or kept behind.

9. Children

Agent 24 is not directed at children under 13, and the project does not knowingly collect personal data from them. The app is not a child-directed service and does not include SDKs that are unapproved for child-directed services. Recommended content rating: Teen (13+).

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated effective date and a new version number. The in-app copy asks for your consent again after a version change. Continued use after a change means acceptance. This page is served as static HTML from a public, non-geo-fenced URL and is not editable by third parties.
