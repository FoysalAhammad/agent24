# ❓ FAQ

---

### 🤖 What exactly is Agent 24?
An autonomous AI coding & technical assistant for Android — a full agentic
workspace with planning, tools, permissions and multi-provider support.

### 📱 Which devices are supported?
Any Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer device with ~100 MB of free storage.

### 🔑 Do I need an API key?
Yes — connect any supported provider with `/connect`. Many providers offer
free tiers. Your key stays on your device.

### 🧠 Can it work without internet?
Session history and files are fully local, but AI model calls need a
connection to your chosen provider.

### 🔐 Is my data safe?
Every sensitive action goes through the `allow` / `ask` / `deny` permission
model, and credentials are never shown in chat or exports.

### 🌐 Which languages does it understand?
It replies in your language — including English, Bangla and Hindi.

### 🔧 Can I add my own tools or skills?
Yes — load custom skills and commands from your configuration directory.
